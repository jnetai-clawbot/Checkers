All fixed and v1.2.1 released as Checkers.apk — same keystore, so it installs straight over v1.2.0.
Ready to test. Release: https://github.com/jnetai-clawbot/Checkers/releases (APK also at apk/Checkers.apk and android/apk/Checkers.apk).
What was wrong and the fix:
1. Root cause — the old online transport relayed a single validated move and onMovePlayed re-broadcast every incoming move back to the sender (echo). Any rejected/echoed/re-ordered message left one side's engine a move behind, so the opponent's pieces stopped appearing on that player's board.
2. Fix — online moves are now sent as a full authoritative board-STATE snapshot (STATE|rules|moveCount|turn|…|board|lastMove). The challenger overwrites its engine from each state, so the two boards can never diverge — both players always see both their own and the opponent's pieces, in every multiplayer mode/connection method (host, join, code, QR, Quick Match).
3. Received moves are no longer echoed, and inbound states are de-duplicated by the monotonically increasing moveCount.
4. The challenger now adopts the host's rule preset automatically, so even two devices with different settings play on an identical board (same size/layout).
5. The host pushes an initial state at match start and after New Game, aligning both boards from move one.
Verified: added OnlineSyncTest (4 JVM tests) proving two peer engines stay perfectly synchronised across hundreds of moves — including when one peer starts on a different rule preset — plus the existing 31 engine tests still pass, and Kotlin compiles clean.
